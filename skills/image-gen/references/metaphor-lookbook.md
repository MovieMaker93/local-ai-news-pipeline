# Editorial Metaphor Lookbook

**Purpose:** Curated vocabulary of metaphors proven to work for Lux in Tenebris ink/watercolor style. Use this when you need inspiration for a specific beat — do NOT copy literally (images would repeat); use as a *springboard* for new metaphors that capture the same ideality.

**Approved ideality → metaphor mappings** (2026-06-27 test run):

| Story ideality | Anti-pattern (AVOID) | Approved metaphor |
|---|---|---|
| Gated/restricted access | "padlock on a server" | Brass keyhole plate on a door made of cloud |
| Knowledge / research | "stack of papers with glowing text" | Cathedral library where every book is stained glass |
| Building physical AI / robotics | "humanoid robot silhouette" | Renaissance artisan fitting ball-and-socket joints into plate armor |
| Multi-agent orchestration | "network graph with nodes" | Creature with 7 heads converging on a central flame |
| Startup funding / capital | "money bag with circuit traces" | Tiny seedling in cathedral floor, one drop falling from vault above |

## Anti-pattern catalog (do not generate)

These visual clichés get rejected by the editorial aesthetic even though they're "about AI":
- ❌ Neural network nodes/edges/graphs
- ❌ Glowing brain, lightbulb head, matrix code
- ❌ Dollar signs, bar charts, growth arrows
- ❌ Generic robot silhouettes or humanoid figures
- ❌ Circuit board patterns
- ❌ Abstract shapes with "AI" text overlays
- ❌ Photorealistic faces
- ❌ Anything with a digital/3D render look
- ❌ Headline → direct visual translation (literal illustration)

## Metaphor rotation — the 6 approaches

Each image in a run must use a DIFFERENT approach. Rotate through these:

### A. Synecdoche
One hyper-specific mundane object encapsulates the whole story. Treat as 19th-century naturalist specimen.
- *Keyhole on cloud door* (access)
- *Single lit candle in a cold city of towers* (isolation amid scale)
- *Worn-out pencil with a perfect point* (precision at the edge of exhaustion)

### B. Anachronism
Concept placed in wrong historical era as if totally normal.
- *Artisan fitting robot joints in Renaissance workshop* (robotics)
- *Medieval scribe illuminating a screen* (knowledge transmission)
- *Victorian astronomer studying a sky of parentheses* (abstractions as celestial bodies)

### C. Scale disorientation
Giant thing tiny; tiny thing monumental.
- *Seedling in cathedral floor* (emergent funding)
- *Microscopic gear train filling the sky* (precision at invisible scale)
- *Ship in a bottle, the ship is the real subject* (contained complexity)

### D. Impossible object
Hybrid creature/structure from mismatched categories.
- *Library of stained-glass books* (transparent knowledge)
- *Clock made entirely of soap bubbles* (time's fragility)
- *Forest where every tree is a tuning fork, vibrating at different frequencies* (resonant signals)

### E. Negative space
What's MISSING drives the meaning.
- *Classroom of empty desks, one exam open with a wet ink smear* (cheating caught)
- *A doorway with the lintel removed, light flooding in* (opening/unveiling)
- *Footprints that stop halfway across a snow field* (abandonment mid-journey)

### F. Allegorical bestiary
Imaginary organism whose anatomy embodies the concept. Audubon/Cuvier plate style.
- *Seven-headed creature at a central flame* (orchestration)
- *Moth with maps for wings* (navigation instinct)
- *Root system that mirrors the tree above but inverted* (mirror architecture)

## The ideality-extraction rule

Before writing any prompt:
1. Read the article
2. Ask: "What's the **abstract idea** this article embodies?" (NOT the subject)
3. Translate the abstract idea into a visual metaphor using one of the 6 approaches above
4. Verify: if someone who didn't read the article looked at the image, they should still pause and think "what does this mean?"

Examples:
- GPT-5.6 gated → ideality is `SCARCITY OF ACCESS` → keyhole on cloud (not "three model icons")
- Robotics milestone → ideality is `CRAFTING INTELLIGENCE` → artisan fitting joints (not "robot standing")
- Funding round → ideality is `GROWTH AGAINST ODDS` → seedling in cathedral (not "money with circuits")

## Visual language (always include in style base)

```
Hand-drawn ink illustration with soft watercolor washes on aged cream paper.
Visible brush strokes, watercolor bleeding slightly past black ink contours.
Warm ivory paper with subtle grain texture. No digital polish, no photorealism,
no 3D rendering — pure handcrafted illustration aesthetic. Dark ink tones 
with restrained amber and burnt sienna watercolor accents. Editorial illustration
style — think Quentin Blake meets vintage scientific plates.
```

Add the metaphor, then append: `NO TEXT, NO LABELS, NO CAPTIONS — pure visual.`
